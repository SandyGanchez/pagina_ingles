02
TYPES OF COMPOSITIONS

Report

A report is an informative text that presents facts, results, or organized information about a specific topic.

REPORT_01 ● ● ●
OBJECTIVE

Inform and Analyze

The main objective of a report is to inform and analyze information accurately and coherently.

A report is based on data, evidence, and research rather than personal opinions.

03 · REPORT STRUCTURE

Three Essential Parts

A clear report usually contains an introduction, a development section, and a conclusion.

01
INTRODUCTION

Capture the Reader's Attention

The introduction is the first part of the report. It introduces the topic and should capture the reader's attention from the beginning.

USEFUL EXPRESSIONS
→ The purpose of this report is to...
→ This report aims to...
02
DEVELOPMENT

Present and Analyze Information

The development is the central part of the report. This is where the information is presented and analyzed in greater detail.

Data, statistics, evidence, and research findings should be included to support the points being discussed.

USEFUL EXPRESSIONS
→ According to the research/findings/data...
→ The data presented in the graph/table...
03
CONCLUSION

Summarize the Key Points

The conclusion is the final part of the report. It should summarize the main points presented in the development section.

USEFUL EXPRESSIONS
→ In conclusion...
→ Based on the evidence/findings...
→ It is recommended that...
