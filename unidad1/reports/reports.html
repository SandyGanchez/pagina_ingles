01

What Is a Report?

📄

A report is a formal piece of writing that presents information about a specific topic, situation, event, or investigation.

Reports are normally organized into sections and use clear, objective, and factual language. They may present findings, explain a situation, or make recommendations.

02

Purpose of a Report

Reports can have different purposes depending on the situation.

🔎
Inform

Present important information about a specific topic.

📊
Present Findings

Show results obtained from research, surveys, or investigations.

💡
Explain

Explain a situation, process, problem, or event.

📌
Recommend

Suggest possible actions, improvements, or solutions.

03

Characteristics of a Report

A good report usually has the following characteristics.

01
Clear and Organized

Information is divided into sections so that the reader can easily follow it.

02
Formal Language

Reports generally use formal, precise, and appropriate vocabulary.

03
Objective

Reports focus on facts and evidence rather than personal opinions.

04
Factual Information

Information should be accurate, relevant, and supported by evidence.

04

Types of Reports

Different situations require different types of reports.

01
Informational Report

Presents facts and information about a specific subject.

Example:
A report about recycling practices at a school.
02
Research Report

Presents information collected through research or investigation.

Example:
A report about students' study habits.
03
Recommendation Report

Presents information and suggests possible solutions or actions.

Example:
A report recommending improvements to the school library.
05

Structure of a Report

A report is usually organized into several sections.

1
Title

Clearly identifies the topic of the report.

2
Introduction

Introduces the topic and explains the purpose of the report.

3
Body / Findings

Presents the main information, facts, results, and details.

4
Conclusion

Summarizes the main findings and important information.

5
Recommendations

Suggests possible actions or solutions when they are necessary.

06

Useful Language for Reports

These expressions can help you organize and write a report.

Introduction

The purpose of this report is to...

This report presents information about...

Findings

The results show that...

According to the findings,...

Conclusion

In conclusion,...

Overall, the results indicate that...

Recommendation

It is recommended that...

It would be useful to...

Complete Report Example

Read the example and identify how each section follows the structure of a report.

EXAMPLE REPORT

School Recycling Report

Prepared for the English Course
INTRODUCTION

The purpose of this report is to describe recycling practices at our school and identify areas that could be improved.

FINDINGS

A survey was conducted among 50 students. The results showed that most students understand the importance of recycling. However, only 32 students reported using the recycling bins regularly.

The survey also showed that some students were unsure about which materials should be placed in each recycling bin.

CONCLUSION

The school has good recycling practices, but there is still room for improvement. More information could help students recycle correctly and consistently.

RECOMMENDATIONS

It is recommended that more recycling bins be placed around the school. In addition, short environmental activities could be organized to help students learn how to separate recyclable materials correctly.
