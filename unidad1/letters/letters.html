Writing in English

INTRODUCTION

What is a Letter?

Learn what a letter is and why it is important in English communication.

✉️

A Written Form of Communication

A letter is a written message sent from one person to another. It can be used to share information, express feelings, make requests, give thanks or communicate ideas.

PURPOSE

Why Do We Write Letters?

Letters can be written for different purposes depending on the situation.

💬
Communicate

Share information, ideas and experiences with another person.

❤️
Express Feelings

Express emotions, gratitude and personal thoughts.

🙋
Make Requests

Ask for information, help or permission in a formal way.

🎓
Learn Writing

Practice vocabulary, grammar and written communication in English.

TYPES

Types of Letters

There are different types of letters depending on the purpose and the person receiving them.

1
Informal Letter

Used to write to friends, family members or people you know well.

Example:
A letter to a friend about your vacation.
2
Formal Letter

Used in professional, academic or official situations.

Example:
Asking a company for information.
3
Thank You Letter

Used to express gratitude for something another person has done.

Example:
Thanking someone for a gift or help.
4
Invitation Letter

Used to invite someone to a celebration, meeting or special event.

Example:
Inviting a friend to a birthday party.
STRUCTURE

Parts of a Letter

A complete letter usually follows this basic structure.

1
Date

The date when the letter is written. Example: August 14, 2026

2
Greeting

The greeting used to address the person. Example: Dear Maria,

3
Body

The main part of the letter where you communicate your message.

4
Closing

The expression used to say goodbye. Example: Best wishes,

5
Signature

The name of the person writing the letter. Example: Jonatan

USEFUL LANGUAGE

Useful Expressions

These expressions can help you write your own letter in English.

👋 GREETINGS
  • Dear Maria,
  • Hello John,
  • Hi my friend!
  • Dear Mr. Smith,
✍️ STARTING
  • I hope you are doing well.
  • How have you been?
  • I am writing to tell you about...
  • I am writing to ask about...
💬 BODY
  • I want to tell you about...
  • Last weekend...
  • I had a great time...
  • It was an amazing experience.
👋 CLOSINGS
  • Sincerely,
  • Best regards,
  • Best wishes,
  • Take care,
  • See you soon!
EXAMPLE

Example Letter

Here is an example of a complete informal letter.

INFORMAL LETTER

A Letter About My Vacation

Example of an English letter
DATE

August 14, 2026

GREETING

Dear Maria,

BODY

I hope you are doing well. I am writing this letter to tell you about my last vacation.

Last weekend, I traveled to Cancun with my family. We stayed at a beautiful hotel near the beach. The weather was sunny and warm.

We visited interesting places and tried delicious Mexican food. My favorite activity was swimming in the sea. It was an amazing experience and I hope to return next year.

CLOSING

Best wishes,

SIGNATURE

Jonatan
