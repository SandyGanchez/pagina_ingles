CONNECTORS

What Are Connectors?

Connectors are words or phrases used to relate two or more ideas.

🔗

Definition

Connectors are words or phrases with which two or more ideas can be related.

They help us organize ideas and establish relationships between different parts of a text.

01

How Are Connectors Used?

There are three important rules when using grammatical connectors.

1

Position

Grammatical connectors can be placed at the beginning of the sentence, at the end, or between the two ideas they connect.

2

Nouns and Verbs

After a connector we can find a noun or a gerund.

3

Congruence

It is very important to use connectors appropriate to the type of text we are writing.

02

Types of Connectors

Connectors can express addition, contrast, cause, comparison, concession, conclusion and conditions.

01

Addition Connectors

Their objective is to complement ideas and add information.

And → Y
Second → En segundo lugar
Also / Too → También
02

Adversative Connectors

Their objective is to express contrast between ideas.

But → Pero
However → Sin embargo
Yet / Even so → Sin embargo / Aun así
03

Causal Connectors

Their objective is to give the reason or origin of something.

Because → Porque
Thanks to → Gracias a
For → Porque
04

Comparative Connectors

They distinguish differences and similarities between people, places or things.

As → Como
Not as / so ... as → No tan ... como
05

Concessive Connectors

They qualify an idea or give a negative aspect of it.

Despite / In spite of → A pesar de
Even if → Incluso si
06

Conclusive Connectors

They are used to finish or complete an idea.

Therefore → Por lo tanto
Thus → Por lo tanto / Así
So → Entonces
07

Conditional Connectors

They are used to express a condition for another idea to happen.

If / Whether → Si
Unless → A menos que
Provided / Providing / As long as → Mientras que / Siempre que
03

Grammatical Structure

Connectors can be grouped according to their grammatical structure.

1

Coordinating Conjunctions

For → Para
And → Y
Nor / Or → O
But → Pero
Yet → Todavía
So → Entonces

Example:

I wanted to go, but it was raining.
2

Conjunctive Adverbs

However → Sin embargo
Therefore → Por lo tanto
Furthermore → Además
Meanwhile → Mientras que

Example:

I stayed at home. However, I enjoyed the day.
3

Subordinating Conjunctions

Because → Porque
Although → A pesar de
Since → Desde
If → Si
While → Mientras

Examples:

Although it was raining, I went out.

I went out because I was bored.
