Third-Person Narration

Learn how to tell a story from the perspective of a narrator who is outside the story.

01

What Is Third-Person Narration?

Third-person narration is a way of telling a story in which the narrator is not a character in the story. The narrator talks about the characters using pronouns such as he, she, they, and it.

HE Carlos
SHE Maria
THEY The students

Example:

Maria walked into the classroom. She opened her notebook and prepared for the lesson.

02

Why Do We Use Third-Person Narration?

Third-person narration allows the writer to describe characters and events from an outside point of view.

👀
Describe Characters

The narrator can describe what characters do and say.

📖
Tell Stories

It is commonly used in stories, novels, and narratives.

🌎
Show Events

The narrator can describe different events in the story.

🎭
Explore Characters

Depending on the narrator, readers may learn about characters' thoughts and feelings.

03

Characteristics of Third-Person Narration

✔ The narrator is outside the story

The narrator is not usually one of the characters participating in the events.

✔ Uses third-person pronouns

Common pronouns include he, she, they, and it.

✔ Focuses on characters

The narrator describes what characters do, say, think, or feel.

✔ Describes events

The narrator can explain what happens during the story.

04

Types of Third-Person Narrators

Third-person narration can provide different levels of information about the characters.

🔍
TYPE 1

Third-Person Limited

The narrator follows one character closely and usually describes that character's thoughts and feelings.

Example:

Emma looked at the clock. She wondered if her friend would arrive soon.
🌐
TYPE 2

Third-Person Omniscient

The narrator can know the thoughts, feelings, and experiences of multiple characters.

Example:

Emma felt nervous, while Daniel was excited about the meeting.
🎥
TYPE 3

Third-Person Objective

The narrator describes observable actions and events without directly explaining characters' thoughts.

Example:

Daniel entered the room. He placed his backpack beside the desk.
05

Common Third-Person Pronouns

These pronouns help the narrator talk about characters without using "I" or "we".

Pronoun Example Reference
He He opened the door. Male character
She She smiled at her friend. Female character
They They walked to school. One or more people
It It moved across the floor. Thing or animal
06

How to Write in Third Person

1

Choose Your Character

Decide who your story is about.

2

Use Third-Person Pronouns

Use words such as he, she, they, or it when referring to the characters.

3

Describe Actions and Events

Explain what the characters do and what happens in the story.

4

Add Details

Include descriptions, dialogue, thoughts, feelings, and events when appropriate.

07

Examples of Third-Person Narration

Notice how the narrator talks about the characters from an outside perspective.

Example 1

Sofia walked to the park.

She carried a small backpack and looked for a quiet place to sit.

Example 2

The students entered the classroom.

They opened their notebooks and prepared for the lesson.

Example 3

Daniel looked at the map.

He tried to find the fastest route to the museum.

Example 4

The dog ran across the garden.

It stopped near the tree and looked around.

08

Third-Person Narrative Example

Read the short narrative and identify the third-person elements.

📖 SHORT NARRATIVE

The Unexpected Morning

Daniel woke up early on Saturday morning. He looked outside and noticed that the sky was clear.

He quickly prepared his backpack because he wanted to visit the town library. When Daniel arrived, he found his friend Sofia waiting near the entrance.

They entered the library together and looked for a book about astronomy. After finding one, they sat near a window and began to read.

Why is this third-person narration?
  • The narrator is outside the story.
  • The characters are referred to by names and third-person pronouns.
  • The narrator describes the characters' actions and events.
