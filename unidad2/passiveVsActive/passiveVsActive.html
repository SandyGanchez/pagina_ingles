Active Voice vs. Passive Voice

Learn when and how to switch between the subject performing the action and the one receiving it!

🏃 Subject + Action

Active Voice

Focuses on who performs the action. It is direct, clear, and common in everyday conversations.

Examples:
  • 🌸 Present: Chef Ramsay prepares the dinner.
  • 🌸 Past: Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin.
  • 🌸 Future: The company will launch a new product.
🎯 Object + To Be + Past Participle

Passive Voice

Focuses on the object or the action itself, especially when the doer is unknown or irrelevant.

Examples:
  • 🌸 Present: The dinner is prepared by Chef Ramsay.
  • 🌸 Past: Penicillin was discovered by Alexander Fleming.
  • 🌸 Future: A new product will be launched by the company.
📊 Verb Tense Comparison Chart
Verb Tense Active Voice Passive Voice
Simple Present They build houses. 🏗️ Houses are built. 🏠
Simple Past The teacher graded the exams. 📝 The exams were graded. 💯
Present Perfect Someone has stolen my bike. 🚲 My bike has been stolen. 🔍
Future (Will) We will deliver the package. 🚚 The package will be delivered. 📬
