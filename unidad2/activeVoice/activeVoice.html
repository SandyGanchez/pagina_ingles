Active Voice

Learn how to make sentences clear, direct, and focused on the person or thing performing an action.

01

What Is Active Voice?

Active voice is a sentence structure in which the subject performs the action expressed by the verb.

Subject + Verb + Object

Example: The student writes a report.

The student performs the action of writing.

02

Why Do We Use Active Voice?

Active voice is useful when we want our sentences to be direct and easy to understand.

🎯
Be Clear

The reader can easily identify who performs the action.

⚡
Be Direct

Active sentences usually communicate ideas in a direct way.

📝
Improve Writing

It can make writing easier to follow and more engaging.

💬
Communicate

It helps express actions clearly in everyday English.

03

Characteristics of Active Voice

✔ The subject performs the action

The subject is the person, animal, or thing doing the action.

✔ The verb shows the action

The verb describes what the subject does.

✔ The sentence is direct

The relationship between the subject and the action is easy to identify.

✔ The object receives the action

When there is an object, it receives the action performed by the subject.

04

Structure of Active Voice

The basic structure is simple:

1

Subject

Who performs the action?

+
2

Verb

What action happens?

+
3

Object

Who or what receives the action?

05

How to Form Active Voice

Subject
The students
Verb
completed
Object
the project.

06

Examples of Active Voice

Notice how the subject performs the action.

Present Simple

Maria reads books.

Maria = subject
reads = verb
books = object

Past Simple

John cleaned the classroom.

John performs the action of cleaning.

Future

The team will finish the project.

The team performs the future action.

Present Continuous

The students are writing a report.

The students are performing the action now.
