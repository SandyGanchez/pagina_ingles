01

What Is the Present Simple?

The present simple is a verb tense used to talk about actions that happen regularly, habits, routines, general facts, and things that are generally true.

It is one of the most common verb tenses in English and is used to describe things that happen every day, usually, often, or always.

Example: I → study → English every day.
02

When Do We Use It?

The present simple has several important uses.

🔄
Habits

Actions that happen regularly.

I exercise every day.
⏰
Routines

Things we normally do.

She gets up at 7:00.
🌎
Facts

General truths and facts.

The sun rises in the east.
📅
Regular Actions

Actions repeated over time.

They play soccer on Sundays.
03

Affirmative Form

We use the base form of the verb with most subjects.

Subject
I / You / We / They
Verb
Base form
I play soccer.
They study English.

Third Person: He / She / It

With he, she, and it, the verb usually changes.

Subject
He / She / It
Verb
Verb + s / es
She plays soccer.
He studies English.
04

Third Person Rules

These are the main spelling rules for he, she, and it.

1. Add -s

Most verbs simply add -s.

play → plays

She plays tennis.

2. Add -es

Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o usually add -es.

watch → watches

He watches TV.

3. Change -y to -ies

When a verb ends in consonant + y, change y to ies.

study → studies

She studies English.

05

Negative Form

Use do not or does not before the base verb.

I / YOU / WE / THEY

Subject + do not + verb

I do not play basketball.

Short form: I don't play basketball.

HE / SHE / IT

Subject + does not + verb

She does not play basketball.

Short form: She doesn't play basketball.

Important: After doesn't, the main verb returns to its base form.

❌ She doesn't plays soccer.
✅ She doesn't play soccer.

06

Interrogative Form

To ask questions, begin with Do or Does.

DO

Do + I / you / we / they + verb?

Do you study English?

Do they play soccer?

↔
DOES

Does + he / she / it + verb?

Does she study English?

Does he play soccer?

Important: After Does, use the base form of the verb.

❌ Does she studies English?
✅ Does she study English?

07

Common Time Expressions

These words are commonly used with the present simple.

always • usually • often • sometimes • never • every day • on Mondays

Always

She always studies at night.

Usually

We usually eat at home.

Never

He never drinks coffee.

08
